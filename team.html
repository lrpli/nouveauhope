Leadership

Meet Our Founder

ML
⭐ Principal Officer & Founder
Mary Lopusnak
Founder & Executive Director

Mary Lopusnak is the visionary behind Nouveau Hope Wellness Group Inc. — a nonprofit she founded in Marianna, Florida with a simple but powerful conviction: that every person deserves access to compassionate wellness support, no matter their circumstances.

Deeply rooted in the Jackson County community, Mary brings both lived understanding and organizational drive to everything she does. Her leadership philosophy centers on dignity, accessibility, and genuine care — values that permeate every program and partnership Nouveau Hope undertakes. Under her direction, the organization has grown from a vision into a recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit that has touched hundreds of lives.

Our People

Dedicated to the Community

Nouveau Hope is built on the talent and heart of people who believe in the power of community wellness — from staff and volunteers to partners and advocates.

WC
Wellness Coordinator
Program Operations

Oversees day-to-day program delivery, coordinates wellness workshops, and ensures participants receive consistent, high-quality support throughout their journey.

OR
Outreach Representative
Community Engagement

Builds relationships across Jackson County, identifying individuals and families in need and connecting them to Nouveau Hope's programs and referral network.

HN
Health Navigator
Benefits & Access

Guides community members through complex health systems, assists with benefit applications, and connects individuals to the right care providers for their needs.

FE
Family Empowerment Specialist
Family Services

Works directly with families facing hardship, providing tailored support, guidance, and connections to resources that help strengthen family bonds and stability.

LSF
Life Skills Facilitator
Training & Education

Designs and delivers practical workshops on financial literacy, coping strategies, self-advocacy, and other skills that build lasting personal resilience.

VA
Volunteer Army
Community Volunteers

A dedicated network of community volunteers who give their time, talents, and compassion to extend Nouveau Hope's reach and impact throughout the region.
