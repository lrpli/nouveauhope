Our History

Born from a deep love for community, Nouveau Hope was built to fill a gap — and it has grown into a beacon of hope for Jackson County.

Our Founding Story

A Vision for Something New

Nouveau Hope Wellness Group Inc. was born out of a simple but urgent recognition: the people of Marianna, Florida were in need of accessible, dignified wellness support — and there was a gap in the community that needed to be filled.

Founded by Mary Lopusnak and headquartered at 2968 Russ St in Marianna, FL, Nouveau Hope was built from the ground up with a commitment to serving individuals and families who face real barriers to health and well-being. The name says it all — nouveau, meaning new, and hope, the driving force behind everything we do.

From the very beginning, the organization operated on the belief that care doesn't have to be complicated or out of reach. With heart, determination, and deep community roots, Nouveau Hope opened its doors and immediately went to work connecting neighbors to resources, providing direct support, and advocating for the well-being of Jackson County residents.

What started as a vision is now a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, recognized by the IRS and anchored in the community it serves.

Milestones

Our Journey

From a vision to a vital community institution — here are the moments that have defined us.

2023 — Founding
Nouveau Hope Is Born
Mary Lopusnak establishes Nouveau Hope Wellness Group Inc. in Marianna, FL, with a clear mission to provide accessible wellness services to the local community.
2023 — Recognition
Federal 501(c)(3) Status Granted
The IRS officially recognizes Nouveau Hope as a tax-exempt nonprofit organization, validating our mission and enabling us to pursue funding and partnerships at scale.
2024 — Growth
Programs Launch & Expand
Core wellness programs take shape — mental health support, family empowerment, community outreach — reaching hundreds of individuals across Jackson County.
2024 — Community
Partnerships & Referral Network
Nouveau Hope builds a robust network of local, state, and national resource partners — strengthening the organization's ability to connect community members to the right care.
2025 — Impact
500+ Lives Touched
A major milestone: more than 500 individuals and families have been served through Nouveau Hope's programs, marking the growing impact of the organization across the region.
2026 — Today
Growing & Going Forward
Nouveau Hope continues to deepen its roots in Marianna, expanding wellness offerings and working toward a future where every community member has access to the care they deserve.
